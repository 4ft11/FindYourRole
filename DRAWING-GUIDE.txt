RUMERIANNE — YOUR ARTWORK, PIECE BY PIECE

You do not need to draw everything before using the website.
The current illustrations are temporary, replaceable assets. All words,
buttons, role selection, messages, and dice remain working website elements.

START WITH ONE THING: THE OPEN BOOK

Canvas: 3000 × 1800 pixels, landscape, 300 DPI, sRGB.
Draw it facing the viewer almost straight on, not steeply tilted.
Keep the spine exactly in the middle and both pages largely blank.
Keep ornament near the edges, leaving the central 70–80% of each page pale
and quiet enough for black writing.

Suggested layers:
  1. Left page
  2. Right page
  3. Spine / gutter
  4. Leather cover, edges, metal corners
  5. Shadow underneath

Export a transparent PNG of the complete open book.
Keep the layered Procreate original. If possible, also export left and
right page layers separately. The live adventure makes the right page
wider than the left; separate layers let that happen without stretching
your decorations.

Please do NOT draw the headings, player names, buttons, or paragraph text.
I will place those on top as readable, accessible, interactive elements.

THEN, WHEN YOU FEEL LIKE IT

   Landscape. Warm sunlight to one side, quiet moonlight to the other.
   Keep the middle relatively dark and uncluttered behind the book.
   Export a PNG without a book or lettering.

2. CLOSED BOOK — 2000 × 2500 pixels, transparent background.
   Separate layers for cover, clasp, metallic ornament, and cast shadow.

3. SEALED ENVELOPE — 1400 × 900 pixels, transparent background.

4. ROLE SYMBOLS — 1024 × 1024 pixels each, transparent background.
   Same line weight, padding, and visual size across all six roles.
   Keep them readable small. A warm ivory or gold ink version works well
   on a dark cloth banner. You do not need to draw the role names.

5. CHARACTER PORTRAITS — 1600 × 2000 pixels.
   Transparent background if they should sit directly on the page.
   A painted background is fine if you prefer framed portraits instead.

6. GUIDE'S SCRYING BASIN / PORTAL — 2000 × 1600 pixels, transparent PNG.
   Separate rim, empty center, and glow. Leave the center empty for your
   live preview. This replaces the temporary basin shape.

7. SCENES, MAP FRAGMENTS, AND DISCOVERIES — 2560 × 1440 pixels.
   These can be drawn whenever the campaign needs them.
   The current live upload limit is 1.5 MB per image; keep your full-size
   original and export a smaller web copy for a session.

YOUR LANTERN

Your current off/on drawings are already used. You do not need to fix
them now. Future lantern versions should retain the same 2000 × 2500
canvas and alignment. Keep metalwork, paper, hidden symbol, internal light,
and surrounding glow on separate layers when convenient.

EXPORT SETTINGS

Use sRGB and PNG. Turn off Procreate's Background Color when transparency
is needed. Pixel dimensions matter more than DPI on a website; 300 DPI is
fine and keeps your originals useful for print. Do not flatten your only
Procreate copy. No animation needs to be drawn frame by frame.

PRIORITY

Open book first. Then whichever you enjoy most.
The envelope, role symbols, portraits, and basin can all wait.
